Who I Am

I started in Mechanical Engineering — which taught me that every system has a logic, and finding it changes everything. I pivoted to data because data is the language every business speaks, and I became fluent fast. At Wayfair, I don't just build dashboards; I build the single source of truth that marketing, ops, and leadership trust every morning. My edge? I can sit with a VP and walk through a cohort retention curve — and they'll actually lean in, not zone out.

I also taught graduate-level analytics as a Teaching Assistant at Sacred Heart University. That experience forced me to go deeper — because you only truly understand something when you can explain it clearly to someone else. That's the communication skill I bring to every stakeholder presentation.

Every dataset is a conversation. I start with the business question, not the data. What decision needs making? Then I work backwards: query → clean → transform → visualize → present.
